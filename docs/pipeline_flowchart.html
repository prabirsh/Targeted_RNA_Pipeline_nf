RNA Panel Pipeline — Flowchart (v1.1)

Clinical FFPE RNA, targeted panel. Blue = tool/process · purple = panel choice · amber = file · green = final deliverable · ★ = panel-specific step.

How to read it: top to bottom. You pick a panel (ST8 or CT) at the start; that panel BED becomes an interval_list and feeds the two panel-specific steps (Picard HsMetrics and mosdepth, dashed purple). STAR is the hub: its BAM drives all QC, coverage, expression and Arriba; its junction file drives STAR-Fusion. mosdepth → coverage_summary.py gives total reads, mean target coverage and % bases at 50/100/200/500/1000x. Arriba + STAR-Fusion → merge_fusions.py gives one ranked table with left/right breakpoints, read support and read names. Two final deliverables: multiqc_report.html (QC sign-off) and fusions.reported.tsv (clinical fusions); counts.txt is the expression matrix.
